Temporary starmap, asteroid and full-canvas previews for Oxygen Not Included: Spaced Out!

Shift + F7: preview the starmap.
Shift + F8: preview the shared simulation canvas containing all worlds.
Select an asteroid on the starmap and choose Preview map to inspect its terrain.
Use the same shortcut again, or End preview, to return.

Configure shortcuts, language and preferences in Mods > Reveal StarMap > Options from the main menu. Preferences apply across all saves. Routine notifications are off by default.

Previews preserve exploration progress, planet visits and the game's rocket navigation rules. World and canvas previews restore your previous camera view and end when a save is reloaded.

For permanent starmap analysis and rocket navigation, choose Permanent survey under Starmap > Action in Mod Options. Analysis is saved permanently and skips telescope work and its Data Bank rewards. Load an earlier save to undo it. Fuel, range and landing requirements still apply.

Includes English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Requires Spaced Out!

简体中文

临时查看星图、小行星地形和所有世界共用的仿真画布。

Shift + F7：预览星图。
Shift + F8：预览包含所有世界的完整画布。
在星图中选中小行星，点击「预览地图」查看地形。
再次按下相同快捷键，或点击「结束预览」即可返回。

在主菜单的「模组 > Reveal StarMap > 选项」中配置快捷键、语言和偏好，适用于所有存档，默认关闭操作通知。

预览会保留探索进度、星球访问记录及游戏原有的火箭导航规则。结束星球或画布预览后，相机会恢复原来的视角；重新加载存档会结束这两类预览。

需要永久分析星图并用于火箭导航时，可在模组选项的「星图 > 快捷键行为」中选择「永久分析」。分析结果会写入存档，并跳过望远镜工作及对应的资料磁盘奖励；撤销需要读取更早的存档。燃料、航程和着陆条件仍按游戏规则计算。

支持英文、简体中文与繁体中文，需要 Spaced Out!
